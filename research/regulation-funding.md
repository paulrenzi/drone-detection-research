# Export controls, legal and funding
{: .no_toc }

{: .warning }
> General orientation only, not legal advice. Get an export-control specialist involved before sharing military-specific designs or data abroad.

1. TOC
{:toc}

## Export controls (US)

- **ITAR** (State Dept.) and **EAR** (Commerce) can apply once hardware, software, or technical data is *specifically designed or modified for military use* — including trained models and target signature data.
- Information that is already **published and publicly available** is generally outside these controls, which is why this site sticks to public sources.
- Sharing with Ukraine or NATO partners may need licences depending on what is shared.

## Legal (peacetime use)

- Recording audio in public may touch wiretap/privacy laws — another reason to process on-device and send no raw audio.
- Passive RF monitoring of communications ([M10]({% link models/m10-hybrid-cueing.md %})) has its own legal limits.
- Radio modules need FCC (or local) certification.
- Aerostats and drones need airspace approvals.

## Funding routes (US)

| Route | Fit |
|---|---|
| **DIU** (Defense Innovation Unit) | Commercial solutions openings; counter-UAS is a stated priority |
| **AFWERX** (Air Force) SBIR/STTR | Small-business R&D funding; USAFE interest makes this a natural fit |
| **Army SBIR / xTech** competitions | Prize challenges, fast pilot contracts |
| NATO **DIANA** | Allied accelerator for dual-use tech |
