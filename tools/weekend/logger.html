How to use it
Not connected
Browser storage is unavailable or full. Export your CSVs now: data on this page will not survive a reload.

Connect
WiFi, from a phone
  1. Turn on your phone's hotspot. On iPhone, switch on Maximize Compatibility: the board only sees 2.4 GHz.
  2. In the Batear flasher, gateway settings: your hotspot's name and password, MQTT URL mqtt://broker.hivemq.com:1883, Gateway Device ID .
  3. Tap Connect over WiFi.
USB cable, from a laptop
  1. Chrome or Edge on a laptop. Phones can't read a USB board from a web page yet.
  2. Plug the gateway in, tap Connect USB, pick its port. Tap again to add the detector, which adds its confidence score.

WiFi mode sends readings through a free public broker, so anyone who guessed the gateway ID could read them. Fine for a backyard test, not for anything sensitive. Keep this page open with the screen on: readings that arrive while the phone is locked are lost.

Speaker test
  1. Pair a Bluetooth speaker with this phone. Silent mode off, Do Not Disturb on, Auto-Lock off. Put the speaker on a chair 5 m from the mic, facing it, with this phone and the gateway beside it.
  2. Pick a sound, tap Play calibration, and use the volume buttons until a sound meter at the mic reads 60–65 dB(A). A free meter app on the second phone will do, before it starts recording. Then don't touch the volume again.
  3. Enter the meter reading, and the reading with the speaker off. Tap Start sequence and walk 20 m behind the speaker. Each play is logged as a Speaker run.
