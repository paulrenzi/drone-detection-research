| 2026-10-23 | **Countermeasures: a laser page, and Countermeasures moves into the main menu.** [The laser]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link countermeasures/laser.md %}) checks the claim that a laser can cut a fibre FPV's line "for a moment". It takes at least 0.1 J per mm, about 0.04 s for 100 W on a 0.08 in spot, and the slack line doesn't snap. It works only on a drone you can see, by tracing a loop around it: 40–200 s from a road post, at $2.4–6.3k a post (est.). Blinding the drone's camera with a $120 blue diode may be a cheaper first step. Feasibility 2/5, with tests L1–L3. New model: `tools/lasermodel/laser_model.py`. The drone's line falls across the wire, and on a tripwire alert the trolley runs and cuts it, about 4 s on average and under 7 s at worst. Best beside a road, where it cuts the line of a drone parked to wait. About $3.6k a mile in parts in a small batch, $9.7–12.7k at our price, and about $600–1,100 a mile a year (est.). Set up by hand, about a mile a night for two people. Feasibility 3/5. New tests C1–C3. | 2026-10-23 | **Jamming, modelled.** A link-budget model, [`tools/jamming/jam_model.py`](https://github.com/paulrenzi/tripwire/blob/main/tools/jamming/jam_model.py), and a new section on [How it works]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link models/line-layout.md %}#jamming).
