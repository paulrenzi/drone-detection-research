| 2026-10-15 | Added [A minute against the alternatives]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link research/problem-check.md %}#a-minute-against-the-alternatives): how much warning each known defence needs, compared at 0 s, ~5 s, ~15 s and ~1 min. <ul><li>Weapons need seconds: a net-gun, shotgun or rifle has about 1–4 s of the drone's flight in range, so a minute adds little over a sensor at the position.</li><li>The minute buys a go/no-go call on movement (vehicle runs, resupply pickups, walkers), which only a forward line gives against fibre-optic FPVs. Russia's roadside drone "traffic lights" already do this job for roads.</li><li>A radio detector already gives a minute or more, but only against radio-controlled FPVs.</li><li>Evacuations already wait hours for night, fog or rain; whether a minute would change that is a T18 question.</li></ul> The tripwire no longer pitches a minute as a cue for shooters. |
| 2026-10-15 | Added [Build or scrap?]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link research/build-or-scrap.md %}) after a fresh sweep of competitors, FPV acoustics, forward sensors, enemy counters and whether warning helps. Verdict: don't scrap, but narrow. <ul><li>Warning time is distance over speed: any sensor at the position gives seconds even on its maker's best range, and only a line 2–4 km forward gives a minute.</li><li>At equal range on every row, our listeners are about 4–12x cheaper than DroneAlert and 17–56x cheaper than NW0 per 3 km line.</li><li>New: DroneAlert, a ~$211 LoRa acoustic module on a battery (seller's claims), so our hand-placed node is no longer cheaper than the market; SKANER 2.1, Arcani HARK, Sound Shield and ZVOOK Tracking added to the [priority list]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link research/competitors.md %}#the-priority-list).</li><li>No measured FPV sound level or controlled FPV range test exists anywhere; a first-principles estimate gives about 100 m in wind, 150–250 m calm, so plan 20–30 listeners per 3 km line.</li><li>New risk and test T19: common jammers already cover LoRa bands, and a LoRa-hunting jammer prototype is described.</li><li>T18 now asks about drivers, medics and resupply: about half of casualties are in the rear.</li></ul> |

Cost per position depends on real range, which nobody has measured for either; T11 and any independent NW0 test decide it.
